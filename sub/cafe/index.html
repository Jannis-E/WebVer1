OUR STORY

Made by hand, by neighbors.

The Corner Cafe started with a very small idea: a place on the block where the coffee is honest and the welcome is real. Maria and Luca opened the doors in 2014 with a second-hand roaster and a handful of regulars — most of whom still stop by most mornings.

We roast in small batches, bake before sunrise, and try to remember how you take your coffee. Nothing here is trying to be fancy. It's just trying to be good, and to be yours.

— Maria & Luca
